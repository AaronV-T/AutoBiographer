Shared addon ideas
  Character Cards: Let players share an optional biography containing selected statistics, notable accomplishments, favourite activities, and a short personal introduction.
  Shared Adventure Journal: Exchange selected events from a dungeon or expedition so participating players can keep a combined account of their adventure.
  Campfire Gatherings: Recognize nearby addon users and offer group-flavoured stories based on their combined statistics, such as total distance travelled or the group’s most experienced hunter.
  Witnessed Milestones: Save another player’s shared milestone in your journal, including when and where you witnessed it.
  Shared Goals: Create party or guild challenges involving quests, crafting, travel, or other activities, and combine participating players’ progress.
  Shared Notes and Reminders: Send notes about creatures, items, quests, or locations to another player, who can choose to add them to their own notes or to-do list.
  Character Snapshot Exchange: Share level-up or raid-clear snapshots so other players can view your character and equipment as they appeared at that moment.
  Reunion Summaries: When participating players meet again, show what each has accomplished since their last meeting.
